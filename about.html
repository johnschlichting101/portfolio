About me

I’m a recent graduate looking for a job in aerospace. I love being hands on, and I’m looking to take on my next big challenge.

I'm a CU Boulder graduate in Integrated Design Engineering (IDE), with an Aerospace concentration and an Engineering Management emphasis. IDE is a design centered engineering degree built around team projects. You start from a user need, work through analysis and hardware, and finish with a business case. That meant owning five projects end to end, and it's why I think about a part's manufacturability and cost as early as its performance.

Aviation runs in my family

My great grandfather was a lead machinist in Pratt and Whitney's experimental shop in the 1940s, and my grandfather, a retired Air Force lieutenant general, was the program director for the B-2 Spirit and F-117 Nighthawk programs. They have set the bar high and I'm here to meet it.
